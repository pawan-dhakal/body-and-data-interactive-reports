What runs through all three

Different subjects, one argument. Each theme below shows up in every report in the library.

Consent is missing

Biometrics taken because you were told to. Sexual content criminalised whether or not you agreed to it. Health data handed to a guardian on your behalf.

The words are undefined

“Public morality”, “social harmony”, “character”, “unsound mind”, “suspicious”. Undefined terms in law become discretion in practice.

Protection as control

Systems built to include people end up watching them; laws written to protect women end up prosecuting them; families keeping a daughter safe keep her offline.

Nobody was consulted

Local government on the NID. Civil society on the IT Bill. Persons with disabilities on the platforms they are expected to use. The pattern is the finding.

About Body & Data

Established in 2017, Body & Data works to enhance understanding of and access to information on digital rights among women, queer people and marginalised groups in Nepal — towards accessible, safe and just digital spaces for all.

Every report here is also available as a PDF and, increasingly, as an accessible eBook. Learning modules built from the same material are in development.

Reading these reports

  • Every report opens in full text and can be switched to plain summaries from the header.
  • Text size scales to 150% without breaking the layout.
  • Footnote markers and glossary terms show a tooltip on hover or keyboard focus; the notes and definitions are also written out in full at the end.
  • Everything is keyboard operable, and motion respects your reduced-motion setting.
